# Road to 5 Live

My live trading journal: from one Lucid Trading 25K evaluation to five live accounts. Updated every evening after the session.

**Open the page:** https://azulixnq.github.io/Funded/

- The five accounts and where each one stands: evaluation, funded, first payout, live.
- My balance after every trade, with the target and the max-loss floor.
- A log for each day with my notes and my discipline score.
- Every trade with its 1-minute chart, session VWAP and bands.

Data: MotiveWave trade report (Quantower since Nov 12), Lucid balances, 1-minute MNQ candles (Yahoo Finance) and a session VWAP recomputed on NQ. A day whose trades are not detailed shows only its Lucid result (`pnl_lucid` in `data/days.json`).
Fonts: Nojah Sans, a subset of Mona Sans (SIL Open Font License, see `fonts/OFL-NojahSans.txt`), for the words; IBM Plex Mono (SIL Open Font License, see `fonts/OFL.txt`) for the numbers.
The black hole behind the page is drawn by `trounoir.js` (WebGL, light bent around a Schwarzschild black hole, amber accretion disk). It turns as you scroll, follows the mouse or the phone's tilt, shines brighter after a winning session and dimmer after a losing one, and the intro dives into it. With reduced motion it is a still image; without WebGL the page draws it with CSS gradients.
The all-time P&L at the top adds up every account and phase since Oct 28; the LucidPro funded account lost on Nov 6 counts at its floor (−$1,000), since the trades of its last days are not logged.
Not financial advice.
